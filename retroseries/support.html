Retro Series Support

Help for the 8-bit baseball game for iPhone and iPad

Questions, bug reports, or ideas? Email retroseriesgame@gmail.com and include your device and what happened. We read every message.

Common questions

How do I bat?

When your at-bat comes up, tap STEP IN, then pick an approach: CONTACT, POWER, BUNT, or TAKE. Watch the pitch come in and tap the field, the timing meter, or the SWING pad to swing. Swing early to pull the ball, late to go the other way.

How do I restore the Team Customizer on a new device?

Open SETTINGS and tap RESTORE PURCHASES, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. If Family Sharing is on, family members can unlock it the same way.

Where are my saves? Can I move them to another device?

Your careers are saved only on the device you play on, in three save slots. They aren't backed up to a server, so deleting the app deletes them, and they don't sync between devices. A regular iPhone or iPad backup (iCloud or your computer) does include them.

Can I play left-handed?

Yes. In SETTINGS, set HAND to LEFT. The buttons and the SWING pad move to the left side.

How do I turn off the music, sound, or vibration?

SETTINGS has switches for music, sound effects, and each kind of haptic feedback. You can also open it from the pause button during a game.
