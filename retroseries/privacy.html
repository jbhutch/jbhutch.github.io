Retro Series Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Retro Series doesn't collect any information about you. Your games and saves stay on your iPhone, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

What the app stores

Retro Series keeps your team, season, and game progress on your device so you can pick up where you left off. That data never leaves your iPhone, and deleting the app deletes it.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No network connections: the game works fully offline.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone.

Apple's services

Apple may give developers crash reports and usage statistics if you've turned on Share With App Developers in iPhone Settings, and if you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple shares any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. That information is collected by Apple and covered by Apple's privacy policy. It doesn't identify you to us.

Children

Retro Series is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date at the top.
